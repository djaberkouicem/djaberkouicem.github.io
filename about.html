About

I'm Djaber Kouicem, a Master's student of genetics at the University of Constantine 1. I'm interested in how heredity, molecular biology, and data intersect — from the mechanics of inheritance to the tools we use to read and interpret genomes.

This site is a simple, evolving space to share my academic background and write short notes as I learn. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to talk research, coursework, or collaboration.
